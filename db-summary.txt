## Core Tables Description

### User & Authentication
- **bakers**: Baker accounts and profiles
- **customers**: Customer records  
- **customer_sessions**: Customer portal sessions

### Business Operations
- **quotes**: Quote management
- **contracts**: Contract documents
- **invoices**: Invoice tracking
- **bookings**: Booking/consultation scheduling
- **consultations**: Consultation appointments

### Sales & Marketing
- **leads**: Lead tracking
- **calculator_leads**: Leads from pricing calculator
- **email_campaign_enrollments**: Email automation tracking
- **email_jobs**: Bulk email campaigns

### Configuration
- **pricing_options**: Pricing configurations (sizes, flavors, shapes, decorations)
- **quote_templates**: Reusable quote templates
- **contract_templates**: Reusable contract templates

### Analytics & Logs
- **analytics**: Business metrics
- **activity_logs**: User activity tracking
- **audit_logs**: System audit trail
